Frequency of Saving Tracked Events	
Minimum
Specifies how often tracked events are sent to the server. "Minimum" sends only when local storage is almost full (fewer network calls). "Always" sends each event immediately (higher accuracy, more server load).

When enabled, students must watch the entire video before they can progress to the next lesson or attempt a quiz associated with the lesson.
